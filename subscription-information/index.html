Ministri Cloud — Subscription Information

Who provides the service?

Ministri Cloud is provided by Yolanda King, trading as iDesign Labs, at 18 Atlantic Road, Birmingham, B44 8LG. For support, contact ministriapp@gmail.com.

iDesign Labs is not VAT registered. No VAT is charged on subscriptions.

Subscriptions and payments

Ministri Cloud offers a Free plan with no payment details required, alongside optional paid subscriptions.

Paid subscriptions renew automatically at the price and billing interval displayed before payment.

Introductory pricing is available once per church. If a subscription ends and the church later rejoins, standard subscription prices apply.

Churches are encouraged to explore the Free plan or choose monthly billing before committing to an annual subscription.

Cancellation and refunds

The church Owner can cancel a paid subscription at any time through the Billing page.

Following cancellation, paid features remain available until the end of the current paid billing period. The subscription will not renew after that date.

The church then returns to the Free plan. Existing information, members, ministries and records are preserved, although Free-plan limits and feature restrictions will apply.

Payments are not automatically refunded for unused subscription time. This does not affect any applicable statutory cancellation, cooling-off, refund or other legal rights.

Privacy and data protection

Churches control the ministry information they enter into Ministri Cloud. iDesign Labs processes this information to provide the service, in accordance with the applicable data-processing arrangements.

Account, billing and security information is processed as necessary to operate and protect the service.

Our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement provide further information about data protection, retention, service providers and individuals’ rights.
