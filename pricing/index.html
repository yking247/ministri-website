Not sure which plan is right for your church?

Explore the Free plan, with no payment details required, or choose monthly billing before committing to an annual subscription.

Introductory pricing is available once per church and lasts for the original first 12 months. If a subscription ends and the church later rejoins, standard prices apply: £19.99/month or £199/year. Rejoining does not restart the introductory offer.

Cancel anytime. Paid features continue until the end of the current paid billing period. The church then returns to Free, with existing information, members, ministries and records preserved. Free-plan limits and feature restrictions will apply.

Payments are not automatically refunded for unused subscription time. This does not affect any applicable statutory cancellation, cooling-off, refund or other legal rights.
